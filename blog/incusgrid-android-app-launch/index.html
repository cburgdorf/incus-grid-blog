IncusGrid is now on Android

Weather intelligence for sailors, now in your pocket. IncusGrid is available as a native Android app on the Google Play Store.

It's 3 AM. The wind has shifted. You're wondering if the bay across the headland would be safer. You don't want to dig out a laptop. You want answers on the device that's already next to your bunk.

A native mobile app has been one of the most requested features from our users since we launched IncusGrid, and today we're delivering: IncusGrid is now available as a native Android app on the Google Play Store.

Get IncusGrid on Google Play →

Beyond the forecast, now in your pocket

Standard weather apps tell you what's coming tomorrow. IncusGrid reveals the patterns they miss: years of lightning strikes, wind statistics, sea states, and terrain shielding, all combined into one map that helps you decide where and when to sail.

With the Android app, that weather intelligence is now one tap away on the device that's always with you:

  • Lightning Activity: billions of historical strikes, processed into a clear heatmap. See which regions are lightning hotspots and which stay calm, month by month.
  • Terrain Shielding: our exposure layer identifies anchorages that are naturally protected by surrounding hills, the kind of insight a forecast simply can't give you.
  • Wind Patterns: prevailing directions and strength distributions for any region and any month, drawn from years of historical wind data.
  • Wave Patterns: see how typical sea states vary across regions and seasons, so the swell on passage day isn't a surprise.

Whether you're planning next season's route from the couch or making a quick anchorage decision in the cockpit, the bigger picture is right there on your phone.

Why a native app?

The web app at app.incusgrid.com isn't going anywhere. It remains the best way to plan a route on a larger screen. But on a boat, your phone is the device that's always with you, always charged from a USB outlet, and works even when your laptop is buried in a locker.

A native app means a proper home screen icon, faster startup, and a smoother experience when you're zooming around the map looking for that one statistically safer bay.

What's next

We are working on an iOS version next, so iPhone users won't have to wait much longer. In the meantime, if you sail with an Android phone, give the app a try and let us know what you think.
